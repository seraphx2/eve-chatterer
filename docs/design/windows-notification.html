Windows notification mockup

What an alert would look like as a real Windows 11 notification when no EVE client has focus. Only the logo, the text and the buttons are ours; Windows draws the card in its own theme, font and accent color, so it can't match the in-game overlays exactly. The logo is where our look lives: the pilot's tag and accent color on the same steel glass as the overlays, with a notch in the reason's color.

How it maps from an overlay

  • Logo: a small image rendered per alert (tag, pilot accent, reason notch), since a notification can't be styled with CSS.
  • Title: who said it and where. Body: the message, up to two lines. Attribution: which of your characters, and why it alerted.
  • "Switch to …" brings that character's client to the front. Dismiss closes the notification.
  • Everything Windows controls stays Windows': Do Not Disturb, Focus, full-screen apps, and Action Center history all just work.
  • Bursts: repeat lines from the same pilot and channel replace one notification (count in the attribution) instead of stacking dozens.
