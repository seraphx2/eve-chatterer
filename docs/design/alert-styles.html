Alert overlay styles

Three ways an alert can look over EVE, drawn from your Drones window: translucent steel glass, sharp corners, thin type, and the three-segment bar. Everything is shown at actual size on a 1920 x 1080 screen. These windows are click-through, so none of them has a button; anything you would click belongs in a Windows notification.

Panel

The closest match to EVE's own windows. One alert at a time, with room for the reason. Best for mentions and for quiet channels.

Shows: pilot, channel, sender, message, why it fired, time left, and the fold count. About 420 x 118 px.

Strip

A single line that takes almost no screen. Made for noisy channels and for people who want to keep their eyes on the fight; several can stack without covering anything.

Shows: pilot, channel, sender, message (one line, cut with an ellipsis), and the fold count. No reason label and no meter; it disappears on a short timer. About 420 x 34 px each.

Beacon

For the alerts you must not miss. Larger type, a wider frame, and one soft pulse on arrival. Use it for mentions and for channels you mark as always-alert, and keep the rest on Panel or Strip.

Shows: why it fired, pilot and channel, sender, up to three lines of message, time left, and the fold count. About 460 x 150 px.
