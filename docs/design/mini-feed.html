Chat feed

A quiet, always-there history of the lines that matched, in a free corner of each EVE window. The pop-up alerts still grab your attention; the feed is what you glance at to see what you missed. It belongs to the client like the alerts do (follows it, hides with it, can't be clicked) and is placed with Ctrl+Alt+O. Concept only.

One character's feed

The default: each client shows its own character's matched lines, newest at the bottom. Older lines fade, the age sits on the right, and a colored tick says why it matched (amber mention, cyan keyword, blue always-alert). The last 6 lines; nothing ever animates.

All characters in one feed

An option for multiboxing: one feed on whichever client you're looking at, mixing every character's lines. The colored edge and tag say whose line it is.
